Our Story

From one truck to a full design-build studio

IXV Landscape Design started in 2011 with one truck, a small crew, and a simple idea: outdoor spaces should be designed with the same care as the inside of a home.

Fourteen years later, we're a full design-build studio with in-house designers, masons, and horticulturists — but we've kept the part that mattered from day one: every project gets a dedicated point of contact and a crew that treats your property like their own.
